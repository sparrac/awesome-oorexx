## Contributors

This section will list all individuals who have contributed entries, suggestions, or improvements to this curated list.

### How to be listed here

You'll be automatically added to this list after your first contribution is accepted:
- A merged pull request adding or improving entries
- A suggestion in an issue that becomes an accepted entry

If you believe your name should be listed and it is not, please let us know by opening an issue.

### Contribution Types

Contributors may be recognized for:
- **Entry Submissions**: Suggesting new ooRexx packages or resources
- **Maintenance**: Updating existing entries, fixing broken links
- **Review**: Suggesting improvements to existing entries
- **Documentation**: Improving README.md, CONTRIBUTING.md, or other documentation

Thank you for making this list better!
