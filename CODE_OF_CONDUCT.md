# Code of Conduct

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for all contributors. 

In all collaborative spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, etc.), we expect:

- **Tolerance:** Participants will be tolerant of opposing views
- **Respect:** Language and actions must be free of personal attacks and disparaging remarks
- **Good faith:** Assume good intentions when interpreting others' words and actions
- **Safety:** Harassment will not be tolerated

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness conduct that violates this code, please report it.
